## Gradients

Two gradients derived from the palette, kept restrained per the premium+serious mood (low chroma, small lightness delta - no vivid or high-energy gradients). Exposed as Tailwind utilities `bg-brand-bg` and `bg-brand-accent`.

### Background gradient (subtle)

```css
--gradient-bg: linear-gradient(135deg, oklch(0.985 0.004 205) 0%, oklch(0.955 0.006 220) 100%);
```

**Use for:** hero sections, page headers, large empty areas. **Not for:** body text backgrounds, form fields, cards.

### Accent gradient

```css
--gradient-accent: linear-gradient(to right, oklch(0.556 0.047 204) 0%, oklch(0.57 0.05 179) 100%);
```

A small hue shift (-25°, teal toward cyan) - deliberately subtle per "serious" mood; this is not a vivid two-tone gradient. **Use for:** hero CTAs, first pricing tier, feature-card headers. **Not for:** body text, form submit buttons.

## Tone and voice

**Corrected mid-build:** the site must read as teaching/equipping, not selling. Avoid "we sell you X" framing anywhere in copy - favor "here's how we help you build/learn/run X yourself."

### Words to use

Confident, understated, considered. Short sentences, generous whitespace. Direct about deliverables, timelines, and price - no vague "book a consultation," show the fixed scope. Second person is fine ("you get..."), but frame outcomes as the client's capability growing, not a transaction closing.

### Words to avoid

No dash punctuation in generated copy (owner's explicit instruction - use periods/commas/restructured sentences instead of em-dashes or hyphens as connectors). Avoid "luxury," "exclusive," "elite," stacked adjectives, and hype words (revolutionary, game-changing, unleash). Avoid "sell/selling" language when describing the offers to the owner or on-site - "help," "teach," "equip," "build with you" instead.

### Voice example

> A 48 hour audit that shows you exactly where your business is leaking time and money, and what to fix first.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**
- Use the shadcn CSS variables (`bg-background`, `text-foreground`, `bg-primary`) in every new/rewritten page. Never hardcode hex in new code.
- Keep the existing `--steel-teal` legacy variable working until every page referencing it has been migrated (tracked in the rebuild plan's "final sweep" step).
- Test every rewritten component in both light and dark mode.
- No dash punctuation in copy anywhere on the site.

**Don't:**
- Introduce a new hex color for "primary" anywhere - the teal stays exactly as it was, just properly tokenized.
- Use "sell," "buyer," "purchase-first" framing in copy - this is a teaching/equipping brand with paid offers, not a sales funnel.
- Mix more than one gradient on the same screen.
