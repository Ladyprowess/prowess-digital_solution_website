# Working on this live product

- When fixing an issue, inspect the complete affected user journey and all sibling features that could share the same defect. Do not stop at the first occurrence.
- Check related links, signed-in and signed-out behavior, error states, and downstream actions as applicable. Add meaningful regression coverage for functional bugs.
- Report what was verified, remaining limitations, and whether changes are local or deployed. Never imply a live fix without deployment evidence.

- Generated business briefs, planning templates and tool results must use the shared printer-style SummaryPreview flow before printing or saving. Reuse the global preview host; do not add page-level duplicates or direct text-file downloads. Preserve purpose-built downloads for original files, CVs, certificates and other finished documents.
- Homepage interactions must address real user tasks and retain entered work while switching sections. Use explicit service labels (for instance, “Build my website”), grounded claims and customer-facing language.

## Product-wide experience requirements

- Treat design, copy, responsiveness, accessibility, payment, email and account history as one complete customer journey. Do not finish only the hero or visible section while leaving lower sections, forms, reviews or downstream actions incomplete.
- Every major page must have a clear purpose and a distinct composition suited to that product. Avoid copying one service-page layout across unrelated services, oversized typography, decorative numbering, repetitive sections, cramped forms and generic template treatment.
- Write customer-facing copy about the visitor's real problem, desired result, deliverables, process and next action. Do not expose implementation language, internal labels, prompts, AI terminology or vague headings such as “Website.”
- Use interactive elements only when they help a visitor complete a real task or understand a real result. Preserve entered information and provide useful empty, loading, success and error states.
- Check every changed page in light and dark mode, desktop and mobile, with particular attention to iOS Safari and installed PWA behavior. Prevent viewport zoom, shaking and horizontal overflow; keep text, icons, controls and cards readable against their backgrounds.
- Each business service has its own complete sales page, relevant copy, specific deliverables, FAQs and an independently managed testimonial carousel. Testimonials and service prices must remain editable in Business Settings.
- Business-service checkout belongs on the service page. Use a spacious full-width checkout, collect the service-specific brief, show editable NGN and USD prices, take the one-time payment, email a branded confirmation and save the purchase in My Account. Do not route the customer to Contact Us to buy.
- Use the shared printer-style SummaryPreview for generated briefs, diagnostics, checklists, templates and tool results. Results must explain the visitor's selections and likely issues, not merely repeat checked labels.
- All authenticated customers use the same My Account area whether they joined through an individual or business journey. My Account must retain saved items, job and opportunity applications, purchases and payments, subscriptions, bookings, generated work, CVs, portfolios, certificates and other customer-owned records as applicable.
- Keep saved-item collections in My Account rather than the homepage. The homepage may show at most two featured case studies and should lead clearly into Business, Jobs, Opportunities and Skills without duplicating entire landing pages.
- Before marking work complete, inspect sibling pages and shared components for the same issue, verify links and actions, and state any SQL, configuration, deployment or external-service step still required.
